# Changelog

## 0.3.4

- The texts of the Website Kit Settings screen are written for the owner of a site: no file names, setting names or
  technical terms in the labels, help lines and messages. What a setting does for the site, and what happens when it is on or
  off, is said in plain words. The notes for people who write CSS are in a collapsed block "For advanced readers".
- Technical details of the page styles (file name, code of the version) are no longer shown on the settings screen.
- The reasons why the contact form is not shown are written in plain words and follow the language of the person who saves
  the settings.
- The messages that explain why a Custom CSS rule is not accepted use plain words.
- The language button now leads to the other language of the right page also when the address was typed as `/about.html` or `/index`.
- The Privacy page has a short neutral sentence that the site remembers the language you choose. A site gets it on the next
  update; a Privacy page that the owner edited is not overwritten, the new text is written next to it as a draft.
- All new texts are translated into Ukrainian.

## 0.3.3

The language button is a short code with the language name as a tooltip and leads to the same page in the other language.

## 0.3.2

The page language reaches the shop pages; menu labels follow the language of the header.

## 0.3.1

The version is tied to the shipped content, and the upgrade path is checked in CI.
